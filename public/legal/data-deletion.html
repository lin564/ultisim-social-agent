Data Deletion Instructions

UltiSim Inc. · Social Desk

You can ask UltiSim to delete any information we hold about you from your interactions with our social media accounts.

  1. Email info@ultisim.com with the subject line "Data deletion request".
  2. Include your username on the platform (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn or X), and a link to the comment or post if you have one.
  3. We delete the matching records from Social Desk and confirm by email within 30 days.

We delete the copies of your comments and mentions, your username and any reply drafts about them. This does not remove what you posted on the platform itself. To remove that, delete it there, or ask the platform.

Social Desk does not sign you in or connect to your personal account, so there is no app access for you to revoke.

Questions: info@ultisim.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
