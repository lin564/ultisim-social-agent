Privacy Policy

UltiSim Inc. · Social Desk · Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains how UltiSim Inc. ("UltiSim", "we") handles information through Social Desk, the internal tool our team uses to manage UltiSim's and QuestSim's official accounts on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and X. Social Desk is used only by UltiSim staff. It is not offered to the public or to other companies.

What we collect

  • Content of our own accounts: posts we publish and their public engagement counts (likes, comments, shares, impressions).
  • Mentions and comments directed at our accounts: the commenter's public username, the text of the comment or mention, and a link to it. We collect these only so our team can read and reply to them.
  • Public news and web pages about our companies, our people and our industries, which we use as background for posts.

We do not collect private messages, contact lists, follower lists or any other data about people beyond what is listed above. We do not build profiles of individuals.

How we use it

  • To draft, review and publish posts on our own accounts. A member of our staff approves every post and reply before it is published.
  • To read and respond to people who mention or comment on our accounts.
  • To produce internal reports on how our own posts perform.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade any information. We share it only with service providers that run the tool on our behalf, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Anthropic (Claude), which helps draft posts and suggested replies. Comment text may be sent to it for this purpose.
  • Render, which hosts the tool and its database.
  • Slack, which delivers internal notifications to our team.
  • Meta, LinkedIn and X, where our posts and replies are published.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep this information only as long as we need it to manage our accounts and report on them. We delete it sooner on request (see Data Deletion).

Security

The tool is reached only over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is password-protected, and only authorized UltiSim staff can use it. Access keys for our social media accounts are stored as protected settings with our hosting provider, not in our code.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you from your interactions with our accounts, or ask us to delete it, by emailing info@ultisim.com. You can also delete your own comments on the platform where you posted them.

Children

Social Desk is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information about children through it. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Platform terms

Our use of information from Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and X also follows each platform's developer terms and policies.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
